## Transfer boundaries

| Area | What can be reused | What must be established for FC27 |
|---|---|---|
| File mods | Package inventory, integrity checks, isolated comparison and rollback | nxnative's `DM_ROMFS_OVERLAY` behavior; FC26 uses `TOTK_ROMFS_MODS` with its own manifest. They are not the same interface. |
| HD textures | Resource-identification and quality-comparison method; a future replacement algorithm if suitable | FC27 assets, formats, mipmaps, color space, views, streaming and memory costs. No FC26 HD replacement has been validated by this investigation. |
| Grass 2K/4K (FQ-E04-G1/FQ-018) | `passdump`-based shader/texture association, visual shader isolation, complete mip export and a future content-hash-plus-descriptor manifest | Identify FC27's own grass shaders/maps and loader integration. Do not reuse FC26 runtime serials, shader keys, hashes or assets; validate 2K versus 4K at FC27's own internal resolution. |
| Rain/weather (FQ-E06/FQ-019/FQ-021) | Controlled `Clear`/`Rain`/`Snow` matrix, dry/rain passdump comparison, candidate shader isolation and separate precipitation/splash/wet-material validation | Discover FC27's own selector states, rain shaders, textures, uniforms and wet-surface path. Do not treat FC26's `RainyDayPP` allocator arena as weather or reuse FC26 shader keys/assets. |
| Shaders and PostFX | The intended visual effect and relevant general renderer correction | FC27 shader keys, interfaces, matching translation chain and rebuilt cache. A preset named PS5 does not supply PS5 assets or motion. |
| Player animation | Repeatable run, turn and kick measurements; separating frame stalls from pose cadence | Per-build code and data consumers, skeleton/clip compatibility, transition rules and gameplay timing. Field names alone are not evidence of complete functionality. |
| Goal nets | Repeatable ball-impact capture and deformation-cadence analysis | The actual deformation mechanism and its parameters. FC26 has candidate net-field descriptors, but no verified solver control or improvement. |
| Flags (FQ-E05, user-required quality priority) | Fixed-camera motion cycles and separate arm-versus-cloth deformation comparisons; FC26's assistant-referee flag was reported as too stiff | Identify the corresponding assets, motion mechanism and cadence in FC27. Do not assume flags share the goal-net solver or that FC26 settings transfer. No flag improvement has been tested yet. |
| Squads | Source provenance, backups, load and persistence checks | FC27 save schema, slot identity, database version and compatible squad file. |

Do not transplant FC26 offsets, executable patches, generated metallibs or saves. Gate any fixed-address implementation on the matching FC27 build ID and record new evidence. Keep independent baseline files; do not modify hardlinked shader-cache copies in place.
